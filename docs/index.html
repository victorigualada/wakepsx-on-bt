Read your controller's MACs

Wake PSX on Bluetooth wakes the console by impersonating a controller already paired with it, so it needs two addresses: the controller's and the console's. Both are stored in the controller. This page reads them over USB.

  1. Connect the controller to this computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Press Read controller and pick it in the browser's list.
  3. Paste the two MACs into Controller MAC and Console MAC on the ESP32's device page in Home Assistant.

Needs Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser (WebHID). Nothing leaves this page. Supports DualSense, DualSense Edge and DualShock 4; DualShock 3 is best effort.
